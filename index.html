Educational network triage tool. Alerts indicate activity worth investigating and do not prove malicious behaviour.

🔒 Privacy Guarantee: Your packet capture data is analysed locally in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.

Only analyse traffic from systems and networks you own or are authorised to monitor.

How to Think Like an Analyst

"An anomaly is a starting point for investigation, not proof of compromise."

  1. Observe unusual behaviour: Let the detections guide your attention.
  2. Identify the source and destination: Who is talking to whom?
  3. Determine the protocol: Is it web traffic, DNS, or something unexpected?
  4. Establish expectations: Is this a busy server where high traffic is normal, or a quiet endpoint?
  5. Look for supporting indicators: Does a host scanning the network also attempt to download unknown files?
  6. Correlate: Check other security telemetry (like endpoint logs) if available.
  7. Decide: Determine whether the activity is benign, misconfigured, or genuinely suspicious.
